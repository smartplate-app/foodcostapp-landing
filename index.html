Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The free plan requires no credit card and you can start scanning invoices immediately. Upgrade anytime if you need more features.

How is Smart Plate different from MarketMan?

Smart Plate offers AI-powered invoice scanning (no manual data entry), real-time food cost % (not just monthly), built-in labor cost tracking for prime cost visibility, and faster setup — 5 minutes vs. 2-3 weeks with MarketMan.

Does it work with my POS system?

Smart Plate integrates with major POS systems including Tabit, Toast, Square, and others. If you don't see your POS listed, contact us — we're adding new integrations regularly.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Smart Plate is a web-based app that works on any browser, plus we have native apps for iOS and Android. Snap invoice photos right from your phone.

Is my data secure?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure and never share your data with third parties. You can export or delete your data at any time.

Can I manage multiple locations?

Yes. The Professional plan supports up to 3 locations, and the Enterprise plan supports unlimited locations with cross-location reporting and consolidated dashboards.
